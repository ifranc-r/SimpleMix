SimpleMix — 100% Open Source

No ads, no accounts, no cloud — just fast one-click mixing.
Load your tracks, order them, crossfade, and download in seconds. Everything runs directly in your browser with the Web Audio API, and the final mix is saved locally as a high-quality WAV (44.1 kHz stereo).
Hard limit: 1 hour — beyond this, generation is disabled to keep your browser stable.
